PROJECT 01 · FLAGSHIP

When bacteria
fight back.

Predatory bacteria turn microbiology into an arms race.

My Master's research in the Huwiler lab investigates how E. coli resists predation by Bdellovibrio bacteriovorus.

I engineered and characterized bacterial mutants to identify determinants of resistance and quantified predation kinetics using time-resolved growth and survival assays.

Microscopy and image-based analysis added another layer: instead of only asking whether cells survived, I could ask what the population was actually doing over time.

01 Engineer mutants
02 Measure predation
03 Quantify microscopy
04 Analyze kinetics
05 Interpret resistance

Genomes as
data.

Exploring genomic variation and population structure in cancer data.

PROJECT 04 · BACTERIAL GENETICS

Small RNAs,
big questions.

Investigating putative small non-coding RNAs in Salmonella motility-related genes.

For my Bachelor's thesis in the Erhardt lab, I constructed bacterial mutants through cloning and phage-mediated transduction, measured RNA and protein expression, and evaluated motility phenotypes using fluorescence microscopy and flagella staining.
